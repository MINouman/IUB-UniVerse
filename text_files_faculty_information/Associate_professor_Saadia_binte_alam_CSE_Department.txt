Saadia Binte Alam, PhD, is an Associate Professor and Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the School of Engineering, Technology and Sciences. She earned her Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from the University of Hyogo, Japan in 2017, and holds two Master of Science degrees in Engineering Physics/Applied Physics from the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh, obtained in 2004 and 2005. Her research focuses on medical image and signal processing, and data science, with an emphasis on disease detection through machine learning and deep learning. She can be reached at saadiabinte@iub.edu.bd, and her office is located in room BC 5007D. Her academic metrics include 118 total citations, an h-index of 6, and an i-index of 4. For more details, visit her profile at https://iub.ac.bd/academics/departments/cse/faculty-and-staff/saadiabinte.
